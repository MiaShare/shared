Lumion · Product Walkthrough

Building & using two new website forms

A school staff member creates a contact form and an AI lead-capture form, previews them, and a real website visitor uses them — end to end.

What you're looking at

This walkthrough validates that a school can self-serve two new website forms inside Lumion, preview them before going live, and that real (not-logged-in) website visitors can fill them out. It was run on our staging environment using a demo school, "Excalibur Auto Mechanics Institute."

We set up two distinct form types:

Form 1 · Standard

General contact form

A normal form a school embeds on its website to collect visitor details (name, email, phone) straight into their CRM.

Form 2 · AI

AI lead-capture form

Collects the visitor's details, then hands off into a live AI chat assistant ("Nora") that can answer their questions.

1

2

A real website visitor uses the forms

✓ What this confirms

  • A school can build both form types themselves — a standard contact form and an AI lead-capture form — with no developer involvement.
  • Both can be previewed in the dashboard before going live.
  • Both work for real, not-logged-in website visitors, whether embedded as a pop-up or via a direct link.
  • A submission creates a contact record in the CRM and triggers the school's follow-up automations (e.g. new-lead notifications).
  • The AI form correctly hands off from the form into a live AI chat session.

Notes for reviewers

  • This was run on the staging environment with a demo school, using test visitor details.
  • The AI assistant's knowledge base isn't fully connected in staging, so the live chat currently replies with a placeholder ("having trouble responding right now"). The important part — the hand-off from form into the AI session — works; wiring the assistant's answers is a separate, already-planned step.
  • Forms are shown here as embedded pop-ups. A direct shareable link option also exists for the same forms.
